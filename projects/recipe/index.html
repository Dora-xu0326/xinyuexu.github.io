Steps to make a Soft Scrambled Egg and Mushroom Sandwich

This process will take 15 minutes to complete.

Jump to the steps ↓

Ingredients:

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 slices of toast
  • black pepper
  • salt
  • olive oil
  • milk

Equipment: a bowl, fork, cutting board, knife, nonstick pan, and spatula.

Steps：

  1. crack two eggs in a bowl
  2. pour a small amount of milk with the eggs
  3. add black pepper and salt
  4. whisk them well
  5. slice the mushrooms
  6. add olive oil to the pan
  7. put the toast in until both sides are ready
  8. put the sliced mushroom in until it changed color
  9. pour in the beaten eggs
  10. wait until cooked
  11. take it out and put it on top of the toast.
