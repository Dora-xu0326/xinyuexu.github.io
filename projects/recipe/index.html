Soft Scrambled Egg
& Mushroom Sandwich

A quick, warm, simple breakfast with soft eggs, mushrooms, and golden toast.

The whole process only takes15 MINUTES

Jump to the steps ↓

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 slices of bread
  • Mushrooms
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • A splash of milk

Equipment

A bowl, fork, cutting board, knife, nonstick pan, and spatula.

LET'S MAKE IT

Step by step

  1. Crack two eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add a small amount of milk.
  3. Season with black pepper and salt.
  4. Whisk everything together.
  5. Slice the mushrooms.
  6. Heat a little olive oil in the pan.
  7. Toast the bread in the pan until golden on both sides, then set it aside.
  8. Add the mushrooms and cook until softened and lightly browned.
  9. Lower the heat and pour in the beaten eggs.
  10. Gently stir with a spatula until the eggs are softly set and no liquid egg remains.
  11. Spoon the eggs and mushrooms onto one slice of toast and top with the other.
